Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my purchases on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open the app, navigate to Settings (or tap on the Premium screen), and tap "Restore Purchases". Ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID originally used for the transaction.

2. How do I manage or cancel an active subscription?

All in-app subscriptions and payments are handled directly by Apple. On your iOS device, go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions, tap CII IF1 Exam Prep, and choose to cancel or modify your plan at least 24 hours prior to the renewal date.

3. Does the app work offline?

Yes. All mock questions, legal explanations, and your personal performance analytics are stored locally and encrypted on your device. You can study anywhere without an internet connection.

Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

At CII IF1 Exam Prep, we prioritize your privacy. Our application is built from the ground up on an Offline-First and Privacy-by-Design philosophy. You do not need to register an account, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, or physical address.

1. What Data Is Processed?

  • Local Progress & Analytics: Your quiz scores, practice exam completion logs, and bookmarked questions are stored strictly on your local device using Apple's UserDefaults storage.
  • No Server Collection: We do not operate external database servers or analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). Your progress never leaves your device.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The app contains zero advertising frameworks, does not monitor your activity across third-party websites, and never sells your data.

2. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

All financial transactions are conducted exclusively via Apple's secure App Store infrastructure (StoreKit). The developer has no access to your banking, payment card details, or billing information.

3. Managing and Deleting Data

Because your study data is stored entirely on your device, you have total control. You can reset your test history in the app settings or delete all local data by uninstalling the application from your device.
