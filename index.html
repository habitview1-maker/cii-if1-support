Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How do I restore my access pass on a new iPhone or iPad?

Open the app, open Settings (or open the Get Access paywall), and tap "Restore Purchases". Ensure that you are signed in with the same Apple ID originally used to acquire the pass. Your remaining access period will be calculated and reinstated instantly.

2. How do access passes work? Will I be charged recurring fees?

Access passes are sold as non-renewing, time-limited passes for 1, 3, or 12 months. There is no auto-renewal, meaning your credit card will never be billed automatically. If you purchase another access pass before your current pass expires, the additional duration is safely stacked (added) onto your remaining time.

3. What happens when my access pass duration expires?

Upon expiration, full practice sets and mock exams lock again, and the free introductory set stays available. Your saved questions stay on your device and can be practiced again once you renew access. You can see your access end date in the app under Settings.

4. How are refunds processed?

All transactions, billing, and refunds are managed solely by Apple. You can submit a refund request directly via Apple’s dedicated portal at reportaproblem.apple.com using your Apple ID credentials.

5. Does the application function entirely offline?

Yes. The entire 650+ question bank, explanations, and your test records are stored locally and encrypted on your device. An active internet connection is only needed when contacting the App Store to retrieve pricing or to perform a purchase/restore operation.

Data Protection & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 1, 2026

At CII IF1 Exam Prep, we prioritize your privacy. Our application is built from the ground up on an Offline-First and Privacy-by-Design philosophy. You do not need to register an account, and we do not collect personal identifiers such as your name, phone number, or physical address.

1. What Data Is Processed?

  • Local Progress & Analytics: Your quiz scores, practice exam completion logs, and bookmarked questions are stored strictly on your local device using Apple's UserDefaults storage.
  • Security Timestamp: A timestamp is stored in your device’s secure iOS Keychain solely to keep access pass durations accurate if the device clock is changed. It is never transmitted off your device.
  • Anonymous Identifier: A randomly generated anonymous identifier is stored locally on your device. It is not linked to your identity and is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • No Remote Databases or Third-Party Analytics: We do not operate external database servers or analytics trackers (such as Google Analytics or Firebase). Your progress never leaves your device.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: The app contains zero advertising frameworks, does not monitor your activity across third-party websites, and never sells your data.

2. Payment Processing (Apple StoreKit)

All financial transactions are conducted exclusively via Apple's secure App Store infrastructure (StoreKit). The developer has no access to your banking, payment card details, or billing information. Your access period is determined on your device from your verified App Store purchase records.

3. Managing and Deleting Data

Because your study data is stored entirely on your device, you have total control. You can selectively reset module statistics or wipe all study data within the app’s Settings > Data Management screen. Deleting the application removes all local data permanently.
